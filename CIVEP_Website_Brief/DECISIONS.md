# Decisions

This file records confirmed project decisions.

## Business

- Working company name: CIVEP
- Primary direction: China → European Union
- Core audience: Chinese industrial and green-tech SMEs / mid-sized companies
- Typical client size: approximately 60–500 employees
- Primary positioning: European market-entry and commercial partner
- Business model combines consulting and outsourced execution
- No public pricing on the website

## Services

1. Market Entry Advisory
2. European Partner Network
3. Sales Enablement
4. Outsourced Sales

### Outsourced Sales
- European salesperson represents the client directly
- Works under the client's identity
- Can cover prospecting through long negotiation cycles and closing
- Not positioned as simple lead generation

## Brand

- Premium consulting aesthetic
- Modern but not SaaS
- Industrial rather than financial-services-only
- Broad industrial imagery, not focused on one niche such as cranes
- Green technology is an important vertical
- Avoid China/EU visual clichés

## Language

- Primary language: English
- Secondary language: Simplified Chinese
- Chinese content should be localized rather than blindly machine translated

## Site

Primary navigation:
- Expertise
- Industries
- Insights
- About
- Contact

Primary CTA:
- Discuss your European expansion
