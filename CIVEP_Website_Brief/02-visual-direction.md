# 02 — Visual Direction

## Objective

Create a visual identity that feels like a **modern premium consulting firm with genuine industrial credibility**.

The target is the authority of firms such as PwC, EY-Parthenon, BCG, McKinsey or Kearney, interpreted through a more contemporary digital and editorial design language.

## Overall visual territory

The website should feel:
- Premium
- Industrial
- Editorial
- International
- Contemporary
- Serious
- Highly credible

It should not feel like:
- SaaS
- Crypto
- AI-generated startup branding
- Luxury fashion
- Traditional banking
- A generic corporate template

## Photography direction

Approximate balance:

### 60% — Industry
- Factories
- Industrial facilities
- Manufacturing lines
- Machinery
- Energy infrastructure
- Ports and logistics
- Industrial equipment
- Engineers in real working environments

### 25% — People and business
- Executive meetings
- Technical discussions
- Sales interactions
- Engineers and commercial teams
- European business environments

### 15% — China / Europe context
- Chinese industrial environments
- European industrial environments
- Ports, architecture and infrastructure
- Subtle geographic cues

## Photography principles

Prefer:
- documentary-style imagery
- strong composition
- natural light
- authentic industrial scale
- human + machine interaction
- cinematic but credible framing

Avoid:
- handshake stock photos
- China + EU flags
- glowing world maps
- holographic interfaces
- fake futuristic factories
- generic smiling office teams
- obvious AI imagery

## Layout principles

- Strong editorial grid
- Large, confident typography
- Generous whitespace
- Full-width sections
- Strong image crops
- Clear hierarchy
- Limited use of cards
- Minimal border radius
- Avoid pill-heavy UI
- Avoid repetitive 3-column SaaS sections

## Motion

Motion should support confidence, not spectacle.

Use:
- subtle fade / reveal
- restrained image transitions
- smooth section entrances
- refined hover states
- occasional editorial scroll behavior

Avoid:
- constant animation
- 3D gimmicks
- cursor effects
- excessive parallax
- loading sequences
- motion that harms performance

## Color palette

**TBD after dedicated brand-direction exercise.**

Requirements for final palette:
- one strong primary brand color
- neutral background system
- strong dark text color
- restrained accent color
- good contrast
- must work with industrial photography
- must feel premium rather than SaaS

Avoid default corporate blue unless the final art direction makes it distinctive.

## Typography

**TBD after dedicated brand-direction exercise.**

Requirements:
- strong editorial display or grotesk for headlines
- highly readable body type
- must work well in English
- must pair cleanly with Simplified Chinese typography
- avoid overly tech/startup typography

## Reference principle

Take inspiration from the clarity, authority and editorial confidence of major consulting firms without copying any individual website.
