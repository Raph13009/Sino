# 01 — Brand Strategy

## Working brand name

**CIVEP**

The name is temporary and may change. The website architecture and design must therefore avoid over-relying on the current name.

## What CIVEP is

CIVEP is a **European market-entry and commercial partner for Chinese industrial and green-tech companies**.

The company combines consulting with operational execution. It does not only advise clients on entering Europe; it can also connect them with the right local specialists and provide European sales professionals who represent the client directly.

## Core market

### Client profile
- Chinese SMEs and mid-sized companies
- Typical size: approximately 60–500 employees
- B2B
- Industrial and green-technology companies
- Companies seeking to enter or accelerate growth in the European Union

### Geography
- Primary origin market: China
- Primary destination market: European Union
- The brand may retain enough flexibility to speak to broader Asia in the future, but the current commercial focus is China → Europe
- The business is not currently positioned as helping European companies enter China

## Industry scope

Broad industrial focus rather than one narrow vertical.

Priority territories:
- Industrial equipment
- Heavy machinery
- Construction equipment
- Advanced manufacturing
- Industrial technology
- Energy equipment
- Green technology
- Mobility and infrastructure
- Industrial components

Avoid presenting the company as being focused only on cranes or one single industrial niche.

## Positioning

CIVEP should be perceived as:

- a consulting firm
- a European market-entry partner
- an operational commercial partner
- capable of outsourced execution, not only strategic recommendations

## Brand promise

**We help Chinese industrial companies enter, sell and grow in Europe.**

Supporting idea:

**Strategy, local expertise and commercial execution — from market entry to long-term sales representation.**

## Brand attributes

The brand should communicate:
- Trust
- Industrial credibility
- Cross-border expertise
- China–Europe understanding
- Commercial execution
- Sophistication
- Modernity
- Pragmatism
- Local European capability

## What makes the model different

CIVEP should not look like a traditional consultancy that only delivers slides.

The intended differentiation is the ability to combine:
1. Market-entry advisory
2. Access to European local specialists
3. Sales capability building
4. Outsourced European sales execution

## Brand personality

- Confident
- Precise
- Pragmatic
- International
- Premium
- Commercially minded
- Operational

## What the brand is NOT

- A generic SaaS startup
- An AI agency
- A lead-generation agency
- A simple directory of European contacts
- A distributor
- A traditional corporate consultancy that stops at recommendations
- A China-themed brand relying on clichés
