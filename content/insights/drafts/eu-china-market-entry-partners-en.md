## Body (Google Doc)

What European market entry requires of Chinese industrial companies is often framed as finding “an agency” or “a partner in Europe.” Searchers asking which agencies specialize in EU–China market entry are usually looking for something more specific: a commercial path that works for industrial and green-tech products — not a generic trade-mission brochure.

## What buyers actually need

European buyers expect clear positioning, credible local support, and a sales process that matches longer procurement cycles. An “agency” that only opens doors or runs events rarely covers that full path. Useful support usually combines:

- Market prioritization (countries, segments, route to market)
- Access to local specialists and partners when a skill is missing
- Hands-on European sales representation for the first deals
- After-sales readiness so early customers do not become a liability

OPOPA is built around that commercial stack — not around being a directory of advisors.

## How to evaluate an EU–China market entry partner

Ask four practical questions before signing anything:

1. **Who owns the commercial outcome?** Introductions without pipeline ownership often stall.
2. **Is the team bilingual and industrially literate?** Europe–China cultural fluency without product fluency still loses RFQs.
3. **What happens after the first meeting?** Representation, specialist coordination, and after-sales matter more than a kickoff workshop.
4. **Can they say no to the wrong markets?** Good partners narrow scope; weak ones sell activity.

## Agency vs commercial partner

| Model | Typical fit | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event / matchmaking agency | Early exploration | Weak conversion to revenue |
| Compliance-only advisor | Regulatory gap | Does not create pipeline |
| Distributor search only | Simple products | Breaks on complex industrial sales |
| Commercial partner (representation + specialists) | Industrial / greentech expansion | Requires clearer ICP and commitment |

If your search query is really “who can help us sell in Europe,” start from commercial representation and specialist coverage — then add compliance and logistics as needed. See [European sales representation](/services/european-sales-representation) and [European experts and partners](/services/european-experts-and-partners).

## A practical next step

List your top two European countries, the product line you will lead with, and whether you need representation, specialist sourcing, after-sales, or a combination. That brief is enough for a serious partner conversation — and far more useful than comparing agency logos.
