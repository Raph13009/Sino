# 06 — Content Guidelines

## Audience

Primary readers:
- Founders
- CEOs
- Export directors
- Commercial directors
- International business leaders
- Sales leaders
- Executives of Chinese industrial SMEs and mid-sized companies

## Content goal

The website must make OPOPA appear credible to experienced B2B and industrial decision-makers.

Do not oversimplify industrial business.

## Style

Use:
- short paragraphs
- strong headings
- concrete explanations
- specific examples
- restrained claims
- business language
- outcome-oriented writing

Avoid:
- generic marketing filler
- hype
- excessive adjectives
- buzzwords
- unsubstantiated claims
- fake numbers
- invented client results

## Claims

Never invent:
- client logos
- revenue impact
- market-share improvements
- number of countries served
- number of specialists in network
- years of combined experience
- conversion improvements
- testimonials
- partnerships

Only publish proof that can be substantiated.

## Industrial credibility

Examples should reflect real B2B complexity:
- long sales cycles
- technical buyers
- distributors
- regulatory requirements
- local representation
- technical specialists
- procurement
- negotiations
- industrial customer relationships

## Chinese localization

The Chinese version should not be treated as a literal machine translation.

Process:
1. English source content
2. Simplified Chinese translation
3. Cultural and commercial adaptation
4. Human review before publication where possible

## Calls to action

Primary:
**Discuss your European expansion**

Avoid aggressive consumer-style CTAs such as:
- Buy now
- Get started free
- Start today

## Insights

Content should support both credibility and SEO.

Potential themes:
- European market-entry strategy
- How Chinese industrial companies sell in Europe
- European distributor strategy
- Sales expectations in European B2B markets
- Industrial procurement
- European regulatory considerations
- Green-tech market developments
- Country-by-country commercial differences
