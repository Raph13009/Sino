## Homepage structure

Recommended hierarchy:

1. Navigation
2. Hero
3. Core proposition / what OPOPA helps clients achieve
4. Services
5. Why Europe / market-entry challenge
6. Industries
7. Why OPOPA
8. Selected insight
9. Proof / credibility section when sufficient evidence exists
10. Final CTA
11. Footer

## Homepage principle

Do not overload the homepage.

The homepage should establish:
- who OPOPA serves
- what problem it solves
- how it helps
- why it is credible
- where to go next

Detailed SEO content should live on dedicated service, industry and insight pages.
