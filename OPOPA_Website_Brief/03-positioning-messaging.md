# 03 — Positioning & Messaging

## Primary positioning statement

**OPOPA is the European market-entry and commercial partner for Chinese industrial and green-tech companies.**

## Core promise

**We help Chinese industrial companies enter, sell and grow in Europe.**

## Supporting proposition

**Strategy, local expertise and commercial execution — from market entry to long-term sales representation.**

## Hero direction

Preferred direction:

### Option A — explicit
**Your European partner for growth.**

We help Chinese industrial companies enter, sell and grow across Europe.

### Option B — premium / recommended
**From China to European markets.**

Strategy, local expertise and commercial execution for industrial companies expanding into Europe.

### Option C — execution-led
**Build your European business.**

We provide the strategy, local network and sales capabilities Chinese industrial companies need to succeed in Europe.

## Recommended homepage CTA

**Discuss your European expansion**

Secondary CTA examples:
- Explore our expertise
- See how we help
- Meet our team

## Tone of voice

Write in clear, confident business English.

The tone should be:
- precise
- direct
- commercially credible
- calm
- knowledgeable
- pragmatic

## Writing principles

Prefer:
> Entering Europe requires local knowledge, the right specialists and commercial execution.

Avoid:
> Unlock limitless opportunities and accelerate your transformative growth journey.

Prefer:
> We help your team adapt its commercial approach to European buyers.

Avoid:
> Our cutting-edge methodology empowers your organization to unlock synergies.

## Vocabulary to favor

- European market entry
- commercial execution
- sales
- growth
- market access
- local expertise
- European specialists
- industrial companies
- manufacturing
- commercial presence
- European sales team
- expansion
- representation
- long-term sales cycles
- local market knowledge

## Vocabulary to avoid

Unless context specifically requires it:
- intermediary
- middleman
- disruption
- revolutionary
- cutting-edge
- seamless
- unlock
- ecosystem (when a simpler word works)
- AI-powered
- next-generation
- limitless

## China vs Asia

The website should be explicit about serving **Chinese companies** because this is the actual niche.

Do not dilute the homepage positioning into “Asia → Europe” merely to appear broader.

The underlying brand architecture may remain flexible enough to expand toward Asia later.
